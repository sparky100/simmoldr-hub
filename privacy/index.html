Privacy

Your information, in plain English.

This website. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them.

The Atlas app. When you sign up, Atlas stores your email address, your name and the models and content you create. It is held in a Supabase database in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Your models are visible only to the organisations and people you share them with.

AI features. Some features, such as importing documents and asking Atlas questions, send the relevant text to Anthropic to be processed. Don't use them on anything you aren't allowed to share.

Open source. Atlas's source code is public under the AGPL-3.0 licence, so you can check how it works.

Your choices. To see, correct or delete your information, email support@simmodlr.app.
