A family of modelling apps

Model the system.
Then decide.

simmodlr is a family of browser-based modelling apps for people who need honest answers about how a system behaves. Each app is built around a different kind of modelling question, and they all share one approach: no install, no black box, results you can rely on.

The approach

What every simmodlr app shares.

Runs in your browser

Nothing to install and no licence barrier. Open the app and start modelling.

No black box

Models are open to inspection, so you can see how a result was reached and explain it to others.

Help built in

Built-in guidance helps you get from a question to a working model, and you can bring your own tools too.
