The approach

What every simmodlr app shares.

Runs in your browser

Nothing to install and no licence barrier. Open the app and start modelling.

No black box

Models are open to inspection, so you can see how a result was reached and explain it to others.

Help built in

Built-in guidance helps you get from a question to a working model, and you can bring your own tools too.
